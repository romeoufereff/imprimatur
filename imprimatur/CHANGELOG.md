## 1.7.0

Lessons from the 2026-10-26 design cycle on two restyled single-file decks (a colleague's 28-slide interactive presentation and its 11-slide exec cut): three parallel critiques, owner rulings, two revision rounds and an in-browser QA script. Each check below hid a real defect until something measured it.

- New `scripts/check_diagram.py`, run by `render_checks.py` (so `qa.py`, the stop gate and brand-audit) on every slide: text leaving its box (a box = a fill or borders on 3+ sides; a line box's half-leading is allowed, so tight badge numerals pass), connectors whose stroke is visible through a line of text (every point tested, endpoints included, no inset on the glyph band; a line masked by an opaque box or filled shape painted above it passes, read from the paint stack), arrow tips buried in a leaf node or floating short of the node ahead (the tip includes the marker's geometry; an arrow with no node ahead is an exit and passes), connector stroke contrast under 3:1, accent stripes under `banLeftAccentBars` (a left bar, or a coloured top stripe over a card — the 3 px stripe cards two critique rounds approved and the owner rejected; a top divider rule is not one), and cards in one grid/flex row that end at different heights (the "one short card"; a card under 60 % of its neighbour spans rows by design). Distances scale with the canvas. It also reports each slide's diagram `vocabulary` (panel constructions — filled or not, borders, rounding; which tint fills a card is meaning, not style — tinted panels, solid/dashed connector colours, everything painted in the decision colour or its tint, text within 8 stage px of a rounded card's edge), which FAILs only when a pack sets `rules.enforceDiagramVocabulary`; values come from the pack's optional `diagram` block, by role (defaults: connector `primary`, exception `muted`, decision `accent` + `surface-accent`, tint `tint`, ≤ 3 panel styles, ≤ 1 tinted focal panel). Calibrated on all 197 templates of the six packs and 87 slides of 8 real decks: the 9 findings left are real (EPAM `41-pipeline-cards` and one client-deck slide draw connectors in the rule grey at 1.35:1; two decks have arrowheads 17–34 px short of their nodes). The padding probe stays a report row: packs ship 8 px card padding on the 1920 canvas.
- `render_checks.py` freezes every slide before measuring: transitions and CSS animations jump to their end state through a stylesheet whose `:not()` carries four ids, so it beats a slide's own `!important` transition rule (the review's first QA measured mid-fade), and running CSS animations are finished. Deck-motion is unaffected (GSAP inline styles). `CHECKS_VERSION` 2026-10-26.1 invalidates every `.qa-cache.json`.
- Screen-reader-only text (an `sr-only`/`visually-hidden` class, or an ancestor clipped to a 1–2 px box) is skipped by the overflow, collision, contrast, gradient and diagram checks.
- Foreign-deck mode in `deck-review` (`references/foreign-deck.md`): render every slide fully revealed, critique in parallel chunks of ≤ 14 PNGs without `plan_check`/`qa`, merge, ≤ 4 owner rulings (depth, accent meaning, layout-only or copy, cover), rebuild in a separate per-slide stylesheet, re-critique once, then QA. New scripts, with no deck-specific class names (navigation, build-step classes, footer row are flags): `render_deck.py` (PNGs, two frames + a delay before each capture, `--chunk 14` writes `chunks.txt`) and `qa_deck.py` driving `qa_deck.js` (box spill, footer row, slide edge, text on text, connectors through text, arrow tips, connector contrast, SVG labels, accent bars, minimum rendered text through SVG and CSS transforms, `--allow` for accepted findings; console helpers `outline`, `spillers`, `setCss`, `cats`). `qa_deck.js` also has `rows`, `pad` (text cramped against a rounded card) and `__qa.typeScale` (try one body-text factor; reads every size before writing, SVG labels included). Tested on the published 28-slide deck: renders pixel-identical to the build's own script (MAE 0.0 on 28/28), QA clean apart from the 2 arrows the review had verified as deliberate and four 2 px top rules on filled bars, left for the owner to judge; on the pre-restyle 9-slide exec file it finds the review's own findings (a connector through "Impacts, conflicts and uncertainty.", route blue at 2.66:1, left accent bars, 12 px eyebrows, an arrow buried in its box). The reference also records the CSS traps of restyling someone else's stylesheet (id-keyed rules, `:not()`/`:has()` specificity, a latent `gap`, a wider brand font in fixed geometry and the type-scale factor, cover `clip-path`, whole-token class stripping, inserting before the last `</body>`, `pipefail`, the one short card, padding for new cards).
- House rules for diagrams, by role: design-crit gains judgment rows (cards built like the pack's card templates — "consistent" is not the test; one meaning per colour; ≤ 3 panel styles; fill follows the legend; top-aligned flow text; one connector colour; density is type size too, one body-text factor after a font swap) and a foreign-deck report shape; brand-audit "card vocabulary" and "accent meaning" rows reading `vocabulary`; anti-slop tells box-style sprawl, accent-stripe cards, accent with two meanings, rainbow connectors, legend–fill mismatch, decorative marks, dense type after a font swap; the designer pre-flight and `designer-bespoke-svg.md` the card and connector rules; `pack-contract.md` the `diagram` block.
- `run_evals.py diagram` (fixtures `skills/imprimatur/evals/stage-diagram/`), a `validate_all.sh` stage: eight seeded defects FAIL naming the element, six look-alikes stay quiet, the fixed slide reports nothing, a deck-style `!important` animation spills without the freeze and passes with it, and the vocabulary rows fire and clear.
- Not changed: the design-system packs (outside the plugin). Proposed separately: EPAM `diagram` block; `enforceDiagramVocabulary` stays off for now (9 of its 92 templates exceed 3 panel styles and 15 use more than one tinted panel, e.g. product-card grids — the vocabulary is a diagram-slide rule, judged by design-crit until the templates are sorted), `41-pipeline-cards` exception path in `muted`, known-issues entries.

## 1.6.6

Two engine bugs from a 2026-10-23 client demo deck (the client's own pack, EPAM co-brand).

- Boot header: `hooks/agent_header.py` (SubagentStart) reads DECK from the spawn prompt's `DECK=` line: the payload's `prompt` when the harness sends one, else the subagent's own transcript (`<session>/subagents/agent-<agent_id>.jsonl`), else the newest Agent/Task call for that agent type in the parent `transcript_path`. Only then `$DECK` and a deck-shaped cwd. Deck folders sit in the vault, outside the session cwd, so the hook printed `DECK=<unset>` and the plugin's default pack, giving the EPAM v3 PACK to every agent of a client deck while the orchestrator's own line named the client pack (the slides came out right only because the prompt also carried it). When no deck resolves but the prompt names `PACK=`, that pack is passed on as `$DECK_DESIGN_SYSTEM`, so the injected header never contradicts the prompt. Replaying the deck's real transcript now prints `PACK=…/packs/<client> · DS_NAME=<Client> Design System`, through either transcript route. Six new cases in `hooks/test_agent_header.py` (5 fail on 1.6.5's hook).
- PPTX export: an `<img>` whose src is an SVG (a `.svg` file or `data:image/svg+xml`, as the co-brand lockup writes the partner logo) goes through svg2shapes like an inline `<svg>`. `extract_ir.py` reads the file, probes it in a shadow root sized to the `<img>` so its own `<style>` classes resolve (Illustrator's `.st0{fill:…}`; svg2shapes reads attributes and `style=""` only) without slide CSS leaking in, and writes each element's computed paint onto it. A `brightness(0)` filter becomes black, `brightness(0) invert(1)` white (the cover lockup); any other filter, a remote URL or an unreadable file keeps the picture, with a note. Until now it was a 1x screenshot: the EPAM mark shipped as an 86×31 px PNG (125×45 on the cover). Scratch re-export of the deck: all three marks are native groups of 6 freeforms (cover white, content `#060606`), svg2shapes verify content-MAE 0.0 / 0.4 / 0.4, no `<img>` SVG left as a picture. `run_evals.py export` gains `img-svg.html` (file with class styles, white filter, data: URI without viewBox, drop-shadow stays a picture); 7 of its 8 rows fail with the new path disabled.

## 1.6.5

Four engine gaps from a 2026-10-23 executive briefing (EPAM pack + client co-brand), each worked around by hand in that deck; each now has a `run_evals.py` grader that fails on the 1.6.4 code and passes here.

- Co-brand: the client mark is sized from the pack logo it pairs with. `cobrand.size_role()` gives a `templateRoles.closing` template cover sizes only when its own pack logo is at least as tall as the cover client mark, content sizes otherwise; `role_for` still decides where the lockup sits, so the content-slide bottom row keeps skipping closings. `check_cobrand.py` measures against the same rule. Until now closing meant cover, so the EPAM pack's `35-closing-thank-you` (a 28 px content mark) got a 48 px client wordmark, ~1.7x the EPAM mark, and both scripts agreed it was fine. A client pack's 56 px closing wordmark still takes cover sizes. `cobrand.logo_height()` reads `height="N"` as well as `style="height:Npx"`, which also feeds the optical-centre nudge. `run_evals.py cobrand` now creates two closing slides: no clean slide may carry a client mark taller than its pack logo, and a seeded oversized closing mark FAILs.
- `log_slide.py --deviation`: a slide keeps one Deviations line, always. `--append` folds the note into that line (`<old>; <new>`, idempotent) instead of adding a second one. The bug report said re-logging did not replace; the transcript showed the revision-round designer passed `--append` itself for its "Audit r1: …" notes on slides 7 and 8, so the 2026-10-06 replace-latest rule was routed around. Reproduced on a scratch copy of the deck's file (1.6.4: two lines each, 1.6.5: one merged line, nothing else touched). Designer and template doctrine updated; new `run_evals.py bookkeeping` replays those calls on a brand-neutral fixture (`evals/stage-bookkeeping/`).
- PPTX export, inline text: words are joined with a space only where the HTML has whitespace or a visible gap between them, so `mig<span class="accent">V</span>isor` no longer exports as "mig V isor". `<sup>`/`<sub>` become same-line runs with `a:rPr baseline` +30 %/−25 % at the parent's size, because PowerPoint shrinks offset runs itself. A word starts a new line only when its glyph box leaves the current line's band; the old >3 px glyph-top test broke the line at a raised ™ and at any larger inline span ("One **larger** word" exported as three paragraphs).
- PPTX export, text over background images: `parseGrad` also takes `to top|right|bottom|left` and colour stops without positions (spread the CSS way), so Tailwind's computed `linear-gradient(to right, rgb(…), rgb(…))` is a native gradient fill. Any other background-image on an element that holds content (a gridline track, a layered gradient border, a photo panel) is exported as a picture of the background alone, shot with the content hidden, and the walk descends so the text, bars and cards on it stay editable shapes on top. Until now the whole element was screenshotted with the content baked in and only a warning was printed; the deck's slides 9 and 12 were split into sibling layers by hand.
- Contrast: `check_contrast.py` and `check_gradient.py` (shared `BG_STACK_JS`) read the background from the paint stack under the text's first word (`elementsFromPoint`, with pointer-events forced on for the probe), not only from ancestors. A label over an absolutely positioned sibling gradient layer is now pixel-sampled instead of failing as white-on-white 1.00:1, so the parent no longer needs a solid base colour. A pale sibling solid layer FAILs with its real ratio (1.14:1 in the fixture), not the white page's.
- New graders `run_evals.py export` (fixtures `skills/pptx-export/tests/fixtures/inline-runs.html`, `bg-layers.html`, exported and read back with python-pptx) and `run_evals.py contrast`, plus `bookkeeping`; all three are `validate_all.sh` stages (bookkeeping also under `--fast`).
- Not verified: how large PowerPoint draws the superscript run. The PowerPoint connector reported a PDF export that never reached disk.

## 1.6.4

- PPTX export: chart or diagram is decided by provenance, not element count. `extract_ir.py` stamps `data-pptx-chart="echarts"` on SVGs inside an ECharts container, and `svg2shapes.picture_reason` keeps those as vector pictures (the documented default; `--native-charts` makes real charts); hand-drawn SVGs convert at any density up to a 500-element safety ceiling (`MAX_ELEMENTS`, was 150). `data-pptx="shapes"` / `"picture"` on the `<svg>` or any ancestor overrides both. The old cap doubled as the chart test and got both wrong: the 1&1 roadmap's knowledge-graph row (180 elements) exported as a picture while `42-data-chart` (a 42-element ECharts chart) exploded into shapes. Re-export: all four roadmap rows are native groups (16/75/180/105 shapes, verify content-MAE 22.8/0.0/0.2/0.2), slide content-MAE 20.1, geometry delta 0 px; `42-data-chart` is an svgBlip picture again, the other templates' diagrams are unchanged. Regression tests in `skills/pptx-export/tests/test_svg2shapes.py`.
- pptx-export `SKILL.md`: text size is px→pt ×0.5 (`PT_PER_PX`, 1920 px onto a 960-pt slide), not ×0.75.

## 1.6.3

- PPTX export keeps each run's font family: `extract_ir.py` records the computed `font-family` stack per text run (and writes it onto SVG `<text>`/`<tspan>` clones, so class-styled SVG labels carry it too), and `build_pptx.font_for` writes the first family in the stack that the pack declares (`typography.familyRequiredPattern`). A family `pptx.fontFamilyByWeight` covers keeps its per-weight full name, so single-family packs with per-weight names export byte-identically (EPAM 8-template export: every part equal). Two-family packs keep their display face: a client executive deck exported every Century Gothic title as Arial; the re-export passes the per-run Century Gothic check on 9/9 slides. Bold is set where the name cannot carry the weight — a declared family outside `fontFamilyByWeight`, or bands that share one name (`{"400": "Arial", "700": "Arial"}`: bold Arial and Noto Sans exported regular before). Regression tests `skills/pptx-export/tests/test_fonts.py`.

## 1.6.2

- PPTX export: `svg2shapes.py` resolves gradient `xlink:href`/`href` chains (SVG 1.1 §13.2): stops come from the first gradient in the chain that has any, and every attribute not set locally (`gradientUnits`, `gradientTransform`, `spreadMethod`, and same-kind geometry) from the nearest referenced gradient; cycles terminate. A zero-stop gradient paints nothing and a one-stop gradient paints solid, instead of `_paints` raising `IndexError` on the stroke's mid stop. Illustrator artwork stores stops once and points the rest at them, so a client deck's cover orbit (`01-cover` r2) and agenda orbit (`05-no-new-platform` r0) exported as svgBlip pictures; both now convert to native groups (verify content-MAE 12.3 and 8.5). Regression tests `skills/pptx-export/tests/` (fixture `href-gradient.svg`), new `validate_all.sh` stage.
- PPTX export: `build_pptx.add_svg_shape_group` removes the shapes it already added when a node fails part-way, so the picture fallback no longer sits on top of stray half-built shapes (the agenda slide carried two orphan freeforms under its svgBlip, 2026-10-22).
- Co-brand: `cobrand.py` detects a white pack logo from the whole mark (a client wordmark's first `fill` sits past the old 600-char window, so the client mark stayed dark on white-logo slides), and `role_for` treats `templateRoles.closing` like `cover` (full-bleed closing slides keep their own logo line instead of failing the content-slide bottom-offset row). Found on a 2026-10-22 client executive deck.
- Co-brand optical centring: a pack may declare `logo.opticalCenter` (0..1 of the logo's height where its letters' centre sits); `cobrand.py` nudges the client mark (`position:relative;top`) onto that line and `check_cobrand.py` measures the centre there. A client wordmark has a `g` descender (x-height centre at 0.38), so box-centred lockups looked ~5 px low (review comment on every slide, 2026-10-22). `cobrand.py --reapply` strips and re-applies an existing lockup so such a spec change reaches finished slides.
